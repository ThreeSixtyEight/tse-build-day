Screens are wonderful at making unfinished ideas look finished. That's exactly why we avoid them at the start.

## Paper is honest

A sketch can't be polished into false confidence. It forces the idea to stand on its own.

## Paper is fast

We can explore thirty directions in the time it takes to set up one artboard. Most of them are bad. That's the point.
