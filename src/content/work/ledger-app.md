Most finance apps shout. Ledger wanted one that whispers.

## Designing for calm

We stripped the interface back to a single question per screen and replaced red-alert warnings with gentle, plain-language nudges.

## A system that scales

The accompanying design system — 80 components, fully tokenised — lets Ledger's in-house team ship new features without losing the calm.
