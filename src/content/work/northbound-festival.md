Northbound asked for a campaign that felt as loud as the line-up. We gave them an arrow.

## One shape, endless moves

A single chevron, rotated, stacked and sliced, became the backbone of every touchpoint — from animated line-up reveals to wheat-pasted posters across the city.

## Built for motion first

Every asset was designed in motion and frozen for print, not the other way round. The result was a campaign that felt alive everywhere it appeared.
