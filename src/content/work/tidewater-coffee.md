Tidewater had outgrown its hand-stamped kraft bags. The coffee was winning awards, but the shelf presence wasn't keeping up.

## The brief

Keep the warmth of a neighbourhood roaster while giving the brand enough structure to scale into supermarkets and cafés across the region.

## What we made

We built a modular packaging system around a single tidal line — a mark that rises and falls with each roast profile. Light roasts sit high, dark roasts sink low, so customers can read a bag from across the aisle.

The verbal identity borrows from shipping forecasts: short, rhythmic, a little poetic. *Fair to bright. Notes of stone fruit. Visibility good.*

## The result

Within six months Tidewater signed 40% more wholesale accounts and picked up three packaging awards along the way.
