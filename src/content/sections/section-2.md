At jazz camp, clarinetist Alvin Batiste asks him to play with his band, the Jazztronauts. It's his first professional gig outside the family band and his first time on the Snug Harbor stage: he's 15, nervous, and sharing the stage with a living legend. He keeps playing with them until he leaves for Juilliard, and other musicians start to take notice.
