He's eight, keeping time on stage with the Batiste Brothers, the youngest cousin in a family already full of drummers. His mother moves him to the piano, the conductor and composer's instrument, the one Duke Ellington played. He takes to it, and the kid who kept the beat for everyone else starts leading the band.
