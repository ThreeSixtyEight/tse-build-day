The 9th Ward's annual second line was halted by violence in November 2024. Three months later, Jon brings a free festival to the Desire area, and when his set ends, he doesn't leave. He hops offstage and joins the Nine Times Social Aid & Pleasure Club in a love riot march through the grounds. The parade that stopped is moving again.
